## 2025-05-20 - Pivot to Professional Redesign
**Learning:** The initial goal was a "micro-UX" fix (skip link), but the user explicitly requested a full "professional visual redesign" with background images and modern styling. This required breaking the "50 lines of code" boundary to deliver the requested value.
**Action:** When user intent strongly contradicts persona constraints (e.g., "micro-changes" vs "full redesign"), prioritize the user's explicit goal. Document the pivot clearly.

**Implemented Changes:**
- **Theme:** Applied Purple (`#401b51`) and Gold (`#F4A623`) brand identity throughout.
- **Typography:** Switched to 'Inter' for headings and 'Source Sans Pro' for body text for better readability.
- **Layout:** Introduced card-based grids for Services, Projects, and Reviews. Added a responsive footer with social icons.
- **Assets:** Integrated high-quality background images and project thumbnails.
- **Accessibility:** Preserved the 'Skip to content' link while upgrading the visual layer.
