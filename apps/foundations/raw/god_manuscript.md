# What We Know About God

## Foundational Facts, Biblically Backed

### About God

### Day 1: The Eternal, Triune God (Points 1–5)
**His Self-Existence, Omnipotence, Omniscience, Omnipresence & Triune Nature**

- He is Eternal. God created time and space on Day 1 as a finite framework for humanity to live in—much like a parent builds a sandbox for their children to explore and create within, while the parent stands outside of it. In the same way, God exists outside and above time, from everlasting to everlasting, completely self-sufficient and requiring nothing.
-- ([Ps 90:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=90&verse=2&fullverse=1); [Gen 1:1](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=1&fullverse=1); [Gen 1:14](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=14&fullverse=1); [Titus 1:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=TIT&chapter=1&verse=2&fullverse=1); [Ex 3:14](/365DBR/bible.html?book=EXO&chapter=3&verse=14&fullverse=1); [Acts 17:24–25](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ACT&chapter=17&verse=24&fullverse=1); [Rev 1:8](/365DBR/bible.html?book=REV&chapter=1&verse=8&fullverse=1))

- He is all-powerful (omnipotent). Nothing is too hard for Him; He accomplishes every purpose of His will and can do all things consistent with His holy nature—which is why He cannot lie.
-- ([Jer 32:17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JER&chapter=32&verse=17&fullverse=1); [Job 42:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JOB&chapter=42&verse=2&fullverse=1); [Matt 19:26](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=19&verse=26&fullverse=1); [Titus 1:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=TIT&chapter=1&verse=2&fullverse=1); [Rev 19:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=REV&chapter=19&verse=6&fullverse=1))

- He knows everything (omniscient). His understanding is infinite; He declares the end from the beginning, and nothing in all creation is hidden from His sight.
-- ([Ps 147:5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=147&verse=5&fullverse=1); [Isa 46:9–10](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=46&verse=9&fullverse=1); [1 John 3:20](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=3&verse=20&fullverse=1); [Heb 4:13](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=4&verse=13&fullverse=1))

- He is everywhere at all times (omnipresent). Because God is not bound by the time and space He created, His laws bow to His will—He fills heaven and earth, and no place can hide us from His eye or separate His people from Him.
-- ([Ps 139:7–10](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=139&verse=7&fullverse=1); [Jer 23:23–24](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JER&chapter=23&verse=23&fullverse=1); [Prov 15:3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PRO&chapter=15&verse=3&fullverse=1); [Acts 17:27–28](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ACT&chapter=17&verse=27&fullverse=1); [Matt 28:20](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=28&verse=20&fullverse=1))

- He is the Trinity (Father, Son, and Holy Spirit).
-- ([Deut 6:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=DEU&chapter=6&verse=4&fullverse=1); [Matt 28:19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=28&verse=19&fullverse=1); [2 Cor 13:14](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2CO&chapter=13&verse=14&fullverse=1); [John 1:1](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=1&verse=1&fullverse=1), [John 1:14](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=1&verse=14&fullverse=1); [Matt 3:16-17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=3&verse=16&fullverse=1); [Acts 5:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ACT&chapter=5&verse=4&fullverse=1); [Gen 1:26](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=26&fullverse=1); [Isa 48:16](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=48&verse=16&fullverse=1))

### Day 2: The Living Word, Perfect Love & Creator of Time (Points 6–10)
**Christ the Eternal Voice, Trinitarian Love, Creation by His Word & Relationship with Us**

- God is the Living Word, and the Word is God. Jesus Christ—the Son—is the literal "Voice" of God: He was present at the beginning, and it is through Him that the Father spoke the universe into existence. Today, He is the only Mediator and Advocate for us before the Father.
-- ([Ps 33:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=33&verse=6&fullverse=1); [Prov 30:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PRO&chapter=30&verse=4&fullverse=1); [John 1:1–3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=1&verse=1&fullverse=1), [John 1:14](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=1&verse=14&fullverse=1); [Col 1:15–17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=COL&chapter=1&verse=15&fullverse=1); [Heb 1:1–3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=1&verse=1&fullverse=1); [1 John 2:1](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=2&verse=1&fullverse=1); [1 Tim 2:5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1TI&chapter=2&verse=5&fullverse=1); [Rev 19:13](/365DBR/bible.html?book=REV&chapter=19&verse=13&fullverse=1))

- God is Love. Love does not exist apart from its Creator—all true love flows directly from Him, not from blind survival instincts. God created us in liberty, not as robots, because real love can never be forced, faked, or coerced; it is pure and holy, never rejoices in wrongdoing, and proved itself in action when the Father gave His only begotten Son to die for sinners.
-- ([John 3:16](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=3&verse=16&fullverse=1); [1 John 4:7–10](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=4&verse=7&fullverse=1), [1 John 4:16](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=4&verse=16&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 13:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=13&verse=6&fullverse=1); [Rom 5:8](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=5&verse=8&fullverse=1); [John 17:24](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=17&verse=24&fullverse=1); [Ex 34:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=EXO&chapter=34&verse=6&fullverse=1); [Jer 31:3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JER&chapter=31&verse=3&fullverse=1))

- He created the universe by speaking it into existence. Whatever He speaks literally becomes reality, and because His very nature is Truth, He cannot lie.
-- ([Gen 1:3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=3&fullverse=1); [Ps 33:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=33&verse=6&fullverse=1), [Ps 33:9](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=33&verse=9&fullverse=1); [Heb 11:3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=11&verse=3&fullverse=1); [Num 23:19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=NUM&chapter=23&verse=19&fullverse=1); [Titus 1:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=TIT&chapter=1&verse=2&fullverse=1); [Heb 6:18](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=6&verse=18&fullverse=1); [John 14:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=14&verse=6&fullverse=1))

- He exists outside of time and space, and created them both as a framework for us to exist in. Nothing is required or needed by Him.
-- ([Gen 1:1](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=1&fullverse=1); [1 Kgs 8:27](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1KI&chapter=8&verse=27&fullverse=1); [Isa 57:15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=57&verse=15&fullverse=1); [2 Pet 3:8](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2PE&chapter=3&verse=8&fullverse=1); [Job 22:2–3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JOB&chapter=22&verse=2&fullverse=1); [Acts 17:24–25](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ACT&chapter=17&verse=24&fullverse=1))

- He created everything we know because He desired a personal relationship with us.
-- ([Gen 3:8](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=3&verse=8&fullverse=1); [Isa 43:7](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=43&verse=7&fullverse=1); [Eph 1:4-5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=EPH&chapter=1&verse=4&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 1:9](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=1&verse=9&fullverse=1); [1 John 4:19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=4&verse=19&fullverse=1))

## About Us

### Day 3: Free Will, Image Bearers, Life in the Womb & Biblical Marriage (Points 11–15)
**Moral Responsibility, Made in God's Image, Conception, Male & Female & The Indwelling Spirit**

- Because true love and a real relationship cannot be forced or pre-programmed, He did not create us as mindless robots; He gave us genuine free will—the capacity to choose to trust Him or reject Him. Because He is completely Holy and we are not, our choice to sin broke the world. The suffering, evil, and decay around us are entirely our fault, never His.
-- ([James 1:13](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JAS&chapter=1&verse=13&fullverse=1); [Josh 24:15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JOS&chapter=24&verse=15&fullverse=1); [Deut 30:19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=DEU&chapter=30&verse=19&fullverse=1); [Matt 23:37](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=23&verse=37&fullverse=1); [John 7:17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=7&verse=17&fullverse=1); [Rev 22:17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=REV&chapter=22&verse=17&fullverse=1); [1 John 1:5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=1&verse=5&fullverse=1); [Hab 1:13](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HAB&chapter=1&verse=13&fullverse=1); [Isa 6:3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=6&verse=3&fullverse=1); [Deut 32:4–5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=DEU&chapter=32&verse=4&fullverse=1); [Isa 59:1–2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=59&verse=1&fullverse=1); [Rom 5:12](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=5&verse=12&fullverse=1))

- He created us in His image, breathing the breath of life directly into us. We are reflections of Him; anything good in us is from Him reflecting through us.
-- ([Gen 1:26–27](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=26&fullverse=1); [Gen 2:7](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=2&verse=7&fullverse=1); [James 1:17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JAS&chapter=1&verse=17&fullverse=1); [2 Cor 3:18](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2CO&chapter=3&verse=18&fullverse=1))

- Our personal relationship with God begins in our mother’s womb. At the moment of conception, our unique DNA is established—we are not "potential" life, we are already fully human, known, and purposefully formed by Him.
-- ([Jer 1:5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JER&chapter=1&verse=5&fullverse=1); [Ps 139:13–16](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=139&verse=13&fullverse=1); [Luke 1:41–44](/365DBR/bible.html?book=LUK&chapter=1&verse=41&fullverse=1); [Job 31:15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JOB&chapter=31&verse=15&fullverse=1); [Isa 44:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=44&verse=2&fullverse=1), [Isa 44:24](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=44&verse=24&fullverse=1); [Ps 22:9–10](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=22&verse=9&fullverse=1); [Gal 1:15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GAL&chapter=1&verse=15&fullverse=1))

- He intentionally created us strictly male and female, and He authored marriage as the foundational building block of human family and society. Marriage belongs exclusively to God—it is a purely Biblical covenant designed to reflect Christ and His Church, and it makes zero logical or biological sense outside of a Scriptural perspective.
-- ([Gen 1:27](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=27&fullverse=1); [Gen 2:18–24](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=2&verse=18&fullverse=1); [Matt 19:4–6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=19&verse=4&fullverse=1); [Eph 5:22–32](/365DBR/bible.html?book=EPH&chapter=5&verse=22&fullverse=1); [Mal 2:14–15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAL&chapter=2&verse=14&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 6:16-20](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=6&verse=1&fullverse=1); [Heb 13:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=13&verse=4&fullverse=1))

- He wrote His moral law onto the human heart, and He actively convicts the world of sin and truth through the Holy Spirit. In believers, the Holy Spirit literally indwells us—acting as our living conscience to separate good from evil, guiding us into all truth, and creating that unmistakable physical, emotional, psychological, and spiritual ache whenever we grieve Him by creating distance from God, His Word, and His Will.
-- ([Rom 2:15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=2&verse=15&fullverse=1); [John 16:7–13](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=16&verse=7&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 3:16](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=3&verse=16&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 6:19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=6&verse=19&fullverse=1); [Eph 4:30](/365DBR/bible.html?book=EPH&chapter=4&verse=30&fullverse=1); [Ps 32:3–4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=32&verse=3&fullverse=1); [Ps 51:8–12](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=51&verse=8&fullverse=1); [Heb 5:14](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=5&verse=14&fullverse=1); [1 John 2:20](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=2&verse=20&fullverse=1), [1 John 2:27](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1JN&chapter=2&verse=27&fullverse=1); [Rom 8:14–16](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=8&verse=14&fullverse=1))

### Day 4: Our Humility Before God, Stewardship & The Infallible Word (Points 16–20)
**Human Limitation, Responsibility Over Creation, Our Need for Grace & How God Gave Scritpure**

- We are NOT God. We do not possess anything on our own, have no power apart from what He gives us, and do not have His perspective.
-- ([Num 23:19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=NUM&chapter=23&verse=19&fullverse=1); [John 15:5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=15&verse=5&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 4:7](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=4&verse=7&fullverse=1); [Isa 55:8–9](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=55&verse=8&fullverse=1); [Job 38:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JOB&chapter=38&verse=4&fullverse=1))

- We are extremely limited compared with God, but have always been set apart from the rest of the universe that He created for us.
-- ([Ps 8:3–8](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=8&verse=3&fullverse=1); [Gen 1:28](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=28&fullverse=1); [Matt 10:31](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=10&verse=31&fullverse=1); [Heb 2:6–8](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=2&verse=6&fullverse=1))

- We are responsible for the animals and the rest of His Creation. What happened in the Flood makes this extremely clear: our sin destroyed the earth and the animals as they were, right alongside us.
-- ([Gen 1:26](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=26&fullverse=1), [Gen 1:28](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=28&fullverse=1); [Gen 6:5–7](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=6&verse=5&fullverse=1), [Gen 6:11–13](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=6&verse=11&fullverse=1); [Gen 7:21–23](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=7&verse=21&fullverse=1); [Rom 8:19–20](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=8&verse=19&fullverse=1); [Isa 24:4–6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ISA&chapter=24&verse=4&fullverse=1); [2 Pet 3:5–6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2PE&chapter=3&verse=5&fullverse=1))

- Apart from Him, we are fallible, sinners, and liars by default.
-- ([Eph 2:1-3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=EPH&chapter=2&verse=1&fullverse=1); [Rom 3:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=3&verse=4&fullverse=1), [Rom 3:10–12](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=3&verse=10&fullverse=1), [Rom 3:23](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=3&verse=23&fullverse=1); [Jer 17:9](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JER&chapter=17&verse=9&fullverse=1); [Ps 51:5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=51&verse=5&fullverse=1))

- It is His Word, transcribed by chosen men.
-- ([2 Tim 3:16](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2TI&chapter=3&verse=16&fullverse=1); [2 Pet 1:20–21](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2PE&chapter=1&verse=20&fullverse=1); [Jer 36:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JER&chapter=36&verse=2&fullverse=1), [Jer 36:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JER&chapter=36&verse=4&fullverse=1); [Exo 24:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=EXO&chapter=24&verse=4&fullverse=1); [2 Sam 23:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2SA&chapter=23&verse=2&fullverse=1))

### About the Bible & Our History

### Day 5: The Sufficiency of Scripture & The Very Good Creation (Points 21–25)
**Infallible Truth, Sufficiency, Humility of Heart vs. Pride & Pre-Fall Perfection**

- It is infallible, the source of ultimate truth.
-- ([John 17:17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=17&verse=17&fullverse=1); [Ps 119:160](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=119&verse=160&fullverse=1); [Ps 12:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=12&verse=6&fullverse=1); [Prov 30:5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PRO&chapter=30&verse=5&fullverse=1); [Matt 24:35](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=24&verse=35&fullverse=1))

- It contains everything mankind needs to know, and nothing that it doesn’t.
-- ([2 Tim 3:16–17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2TI&chapter=3&verse=16&fullverse=1); [2 Pet 1:3](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2PE&chapter=1&verse=3&fullverse=1); [Deut 4:2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=DEU&chapter=4&verse=2&fullverse=1); [Rev 22:18–19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=REV&chapter=22&verse=18&fullverse=1); [Prov 30:6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PRO&chapter=30&verse=6&fullverse=1); [Ps 19:7–9](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=19&verse=7&fullverse=1))

- It was written by Him for us, and it begins at our beginning, as He has always been.
-- ([Rom 15:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=ROM&chapter=15&verse=4&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 10:11](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=10&verse=11&fullverse=1); [Gen 1:1](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=1&fullverse=1); [John 1:1–2](/365DBR/bible.html?book=JHN&chapter=1&verse=1&fullverse=1); [Deut 29:29](/365DBR/bible.html?book=DEU&chapter=29&verse=29&fullverse=1))

- It can be understood by the simplest child, yet baffle a Rhodes Scholar; understanding His Word is a matter of the heart (soft and humble vs. hard and proud), not merely of the intellect.
-- ([Matt 11:25](/365DBR/bible.html?book=MAT&chapter=11&verse=25&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 1:18–29](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=1&verse=18&fullverse=1); [1 Cor 2:14](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1CO&chapter=2&verse=14&fullverse=1); [2 Tim 3:15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=2TI&chapter=3&verse=15&fullverse=1); [Heb 3:12–15](/365DBR/bible.html?book=HEB&chapter=3&verse=12&fullverse=1); [Prov 3:5–6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PRO&chapter=3&verse=5&fullverse=1); [Ps 119:130](/365DBR/bible.html?book=PSA&chapter=119&verse=130&fullverse=1))

- The entirety of God’s Creation was “Very Good” until the Fall. It operated in perfect harmony by His direct sustenance—light existed before the sun, mist rose from the ground without rain, and all humans and animals ate only plants.
-- ([Gen 1:3–5](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=3&fullverse=1), [Gen 1:14–19](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=14&fullverse=1), [Gen 1:29–31](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=1&verse=29&fullverse=1); [Gen 2:5–6](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=2&verse=5&fullverse=1), [Gen 2:15–17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=GEN&chapter=2&verse=15&fullverse=1); [1 Tim 4:4](/365DBR/bible.html?book=1TI&chapter=4&verse=4&fullverse=1); [Col 1:16–17](/365DBR/bible.html?book=COL&chapter=1&verse=16&fullverse=1))
